How it works

Real AI, on your device

Real-ESRGAN — a model trained on millions of photos to rebuild detail that normal resizing just blurs — runs in a background worker in your browser, using your GPU when it can (WebGPU) and your CPU otherwise. Your image is never sent anywhere.

The honest limits of free

There is no server, no account and no payment — so the speed is your hardware's speed. A strong laptop finishes in seconds; an older phone may need a few minutes, and very large results are capped to what your device can safely hold. The wait is the price.

What 8K really means

Upscaling to 8K produces an 8K-sized image with AI-rebuilt detail. It cannot recover detail the original never captured — a tiny, blurry source will look cleaner and larger, not magically sharp. Judge it with the before/after slider and zoom.
